# Chronicare — Chronic Disease Monitoring App

**Chronicare** is a healthcare project focused on helping patients manage chronic conditions and helping care teams follow patient progress through a clear, accessible digital experience.

> **Project status:** In development. Features and screens may change as the team works on the project.

## 🎯 Project Goals

- Make important health information easier for patients to review.
- Support regular tracking of health measurements and symptoms.
- Help patients stay aware of medications and upcoming appointments.
- Present patient progress in a clear dashboard that can support conversations with healthcare professionals.
- Keep the user experience simple, accessible, and easy to navigate.

## ✨ Planned Features

- **User access:** Sign in and account registration.
- **Patient profile:** Personal and relevant health information.
- **Home dashboard:** A summary of recent readings, reminders, and health activity.
- **Health tracking:** Record and review measurements relevant to a patient's condition.
- **Medication reminders:** View medication schedules and reminders.
- **Appointments:** Review upcoming appointments.
- **Progress history:** View past readings and trends over time.
- **Notifications:** Highlight reminders or readings that may need attention.

*The final feature set depends on project requirements and implementation.*
